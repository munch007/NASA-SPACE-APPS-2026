# Project OrbitFlare: Burning Activity Calendar

NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, Pune local event. Challenge: **Harmonization of MODIS and VIIRS Hot Spots**.

A single-page web app that joins NASA's two satellite fire-hotspot records, MODIS (2000 onward) and VIIRS (2012 onward), into one consistent history and shows it as a burning activity calendar for a chosen area.

## How to use

Open `index.html` in any browser. The data is embedded in the file, so it works offline.

- Drag a box on the map, or type latitude and longitude, to choose an area.
- Choose a date range.
- Read the calendar (one row per year, one column per month), switch to "Versus usual", and choose whether "usual" means every year (2003 to 2025) or the last 10 years (2016 to 2025).
- Download the hotspots or a monthly summary as CSV.

## Method

1. Keep VIIRS detections rated nominal or high and MODIS detections at 30% confidence or more.
2. Before February 2012, scale each MODIS hotspot by a factor for its calendar month (4.8 in January up to 13.1 in June), fitted on the 167 months when MODIS and VIIRS both ran (February 2012 to December 2025). VIIRS counted about 6.1 times as many hotspots as MODIS overall.
3. From February 2012 use VIIRS: S-NPP until March 2018, then NOAA-20 divided by 1.007 to match S-NPP. NOAA-21 is used only as a cross-check.

Test: with one year held out at a time, scale factors were refitted and used to predict that year's VIIRS counts from MODIS alone. Monthly correlation 0.97, average error in yearly totals 8%, average error in November to May months 18%.

## Data

NASA FIRMS active fire archive and near-real-time files, downloaded 13 November 2026, for 73 to 75.4 degrees east and 18.7 to 20.5 degrees north (Pune to Nashik region): MODIS C6.1, VIIRS S-NPP, VIIRS NOAA-20 and VIIRS NOAA-21.
https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/tools/firms

203,097 detections were downloaded and 88,595 were used after filtering and removing double counts between satellites. Recent months use near-real-time data, which NASA later replaces with standard-quality data.

## Limits

- Scale factors were fitted on the whole study area and applied to any sub-area.
- Hotspots are satellite detections, not confirmed fires or burned area.
- 2000 to 2002 is left out because MODIS Aqua only began in July 2002.

## Tools

Python (pandas, NumPy) for the analysis. The app is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no outside libraries or servers.
